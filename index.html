On air

    Send to Apple Music or YouTube Music

    Apple and YouTube don't take playlist imports from a web page directly, so the fastest route is a free transfer service that reads plain text.

    1. Click Copy track list.
    2. Open TuneMyMusic and choose Free text as the source (or Soundiiz › Import › From text).
    3. Paste, check the matches, then pick Apple Music or YouTube Music as the destination.

    Or add by hand

    Each track has Apple, YT Music and YouTube links that open a search for that song. Tap through and add each one to a playlist in the app.
